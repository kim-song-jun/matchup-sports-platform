Show nicknames instead of real names in tournament match records by default, with an opt-in profile toggle for players who want their real name shown. Real names are still collected — eligibility review and the registered/unregistered-player distinction genuinely need them — but they are no longer published without a basis for it: the tournament privacy consent's ten stated purposes never covered public disclosure.

Consent for that disclosure now lives in its own optional policy alongside the required privacy one, so declining it costs a player nothing but the name on the scoreboard. Accepting it turns the profile toggle on; existing players who only ever saw the old consent keep their records under a nickname until they choose otherwise.
